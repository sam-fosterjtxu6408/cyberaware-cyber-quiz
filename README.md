## Learn with CyberAware

CyberAware gives students and other learners a practical way to strengthen everyday online safety skills. Its quiz-driven activities present recognizable digital threats, helping players identify warning signs and understand the result of each choice through immediate feedback.

Rather than concentrating on abstract cybersecurity concepts, the game uses applied scenarios involving phishing messages, OTP scams, fraudulent QR codes, weak passwords, and unsafe links. Built-in progress tracking lets users monitor their movement through the learning activities.

## What It Includes

- Quiz activities based on common online safety choices

- Progress tracking throughout the learning experience
- Scenarios modeled on real-world cyber threats

- Lessons on the dangers of weak passwords
- Activities for spotting unsafe links

## How to Use It

To complete a typical session:

1. Open CyberAware in your browser.
2. Select a quiz or cyber threat scenario.
3. Consider the details of the situation.
4. Choose the option that represents safer online behavior.
5. Check the feedback provided for your answer.
6. Continue with the activities and monitor your progress.

The scenarios are intended to build familiarity with suspicious messages, risky URLs, questionable QR codes, OTP scams, and poor password habits.
